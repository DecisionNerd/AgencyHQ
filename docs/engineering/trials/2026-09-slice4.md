## Observations

- The adversarial reviewer reads verification stderr and refuses a criterion whose check is a placeholder (`echo typecheck-skipped-in-fixture`). Three fixture runs were blocked until both fixtures received a real typecheck. This is the F-13 gap closing itself from the review side.
- A contract-class failure (null commit) with budget remaining does not retry automatically. The operator re-plans (plan command) or issues a disposition. This is documented behavior, not a defect.
- Lead structured-output failures occurred 3 times in approximately 23 plan/review runs (parse failure on criteria text; "session prompt failed: {}"). The bounded retry (`e66cca7`) now covers these.
- The trial repointed `projects.allowed_refs.main` by hand three times before the auto-advance fix. After `39a5f7d` the ledger advanced automatically (items 8e, 10g).
- Manifest `targetRef` is the allowed_refs key ("main"); single-repo contracts now freeze the same short form.
- `DEPLOY_NOT_SUPPORTED` was returned for a test contract with `boundary=deploy`; deploy is not implemented.
